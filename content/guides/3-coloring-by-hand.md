# Coloring by hand

The coloring tool lets you paint cells to follow a chain of reasoning across the board. It is made for Simple Coloring, and it helps with any chain.

- Turn on Colour. The number pad now picks a digit to focus on instead of entering one.
- Tap the digit you are studying. Every cell that holds it, or could still hold it, lights up.
- Look for conjugate pairs: a row, column or box where the lit cells number exactly two.
- Select one cell of a pair and paint it with the first colour. Paint its partner with the second.
- Follow the chain: wherever a painted cell is also in another conjugate pair, paint that partner with the opposite colour. Keep going until the chain ends.
- Check the two rules. If two cells of one colour see each other (color wrap), that colour is false: remove the digit from all its cells. If an unpainted cell with the digit sees both colours (color trap), remove it there.
- Clear all when you are done, or undo the painting step by step.

Two more colours are there for a second chain, or to mark cells you are unsure of. Paint is saved with the game, like your notes.
