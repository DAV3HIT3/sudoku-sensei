# Keeping notes accurate

Every advanced technique reads the notes, so one wrong pencil mark can send you down a false trail. Three habits keep them trustworthy.

Remove as you go. When a technique eliminates a candidate, take it out straight away, before looking for the next pattern. The board removes a placed digit from its peers for you, but eliminations are yours to make: select every cell the step names and tap the digit once.

Never remove a candidate on a hunch. A note leaves only when a rule rules it out. If you are guessing, you are no longer solving by logic, and a mistake will surface much later, far from its cause.

Refill when in doubt. If the notes look wrong, erase them (select the cells, then Erase) and use Fill notes to start again from the digits on the board. You lose the eliminations you had made, but the techniques will find them again.

A hint checks your notes first. If a pencil mark rules out the digit that belongs in a cell, the hint says so before anything else.
