# Skyscraper

Take a digit that has exactly two possible cells in each of two rows. If one end of each pair shares a column (the base), the other two ends (the tops) cannot both be empty of the digit: if one top is not the digit, its row puts it at its base, which rules out the other base, so the other top is the digit.

At least one top holds the digit, so any cell that sees both tops cannot. The same works with rows and columns swapped. It is an X-Wing with one corner out of line.
