# Finned Swordfish

A Swordfish with fins: in three rows a digit fits in three columns, apart from a few extra cells, all in one box.

As with the Finned X-Wing: either a fin holds the digit, or the Swordfish is real. A cell in one of the three columns that is also in the fins' box loses the digit in both cases. Sashimi when a row has only one cell left inside the three columns.

Look for these when a Swordfish nearly fits but one row spills one or two candidates into a neighbouring column within the same box.
