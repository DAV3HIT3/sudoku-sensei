# Finned Swordfish

A Swordfish with a fin: in three rows a digit can go only in the same three columns, apart from one or two extra cells, all in one box.

As with the Finned X-Wing: either the fin holds the digit, or the Swordfish is real. A cell that is in one of the three columns and also in the fin's box loses the digit either way. It is sashimi when one row has only one cell left inside the three columns.

Look for these when a Swordfish nearly fits, but one row spills a candidate or two into a neighboring column of the same box.
