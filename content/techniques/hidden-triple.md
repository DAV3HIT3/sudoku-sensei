# Hidden Triple

Suppose that in a row, column or box, three digits can go only in the same three cells: no other cell of the unit has any of them as a candidate. The unit needs all three digits, and these three cells are the only places for them, so the three cells hold those three digits, one each.

Those cells are then full, so any other candidates in them can be removed.

A digit doesn't need to be a candidate in all three cells. What matters is that none of the three digits can go anywhere else in the unit.
