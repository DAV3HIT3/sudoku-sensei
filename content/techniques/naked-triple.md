# Naked Triple

Suppose three cells in the same row, column or box have only three different digits among all their candidates, for example {1,2}, {2,3} and {1,3}. Three cells need three different digits, and these three are the only ones they can use, so the three digits go in these three cells, one each.

That uses up those digits in the unit. No other cell in that row, column or box can hold any of them: remove all three digits from the unit's other cells.

A cell doesn't need all three digits as candidates. {1,2}, {2,3} and {1,3} is a naked triple, and so is {1,2,3}, {1,2} and {2,3}.
