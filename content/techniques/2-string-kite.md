# 2-String Kite

Take a digit that can go in only two cells of some row, and only two cells of some column. Suppose one cell of the row pair and one cell of the column pair are in the same box (and are different cells).

Those two cells see each other, so at most one of them holds the digit. So in the row or in the column, the digit is at the other end: one of the two far ends holds it.

Any cell that sees both far ends cannot hold the digit.
