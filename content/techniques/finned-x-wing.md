# Finned X-Wing

An X-Wing that is almost there. In two rows a digit can go only in the same two columns, except that one row also has one or two extra cells (the fin), all in one box.

Either the fin holds the digit or it does not. If it does not, the X-Wing is real, and the two columns get the digit from these two rows and nowhere else. If the fin does hold it, no other cell in the fin's box can. So a cell that is in one of the two columns and also in the fin's box loses the digit either way.

A sashimi X-Wing is the same with one corner missing: one row has the digit in only one of the two columns, plus the fin. The reasoning, and the cells it clears, are the same.

Rows and columns can swap roles, as with every fish.
