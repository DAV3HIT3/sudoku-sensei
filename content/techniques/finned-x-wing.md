# Finned X-Wing

An X-Wing that is almost there. In two rows a digit fits in the same two columns, except that one row has an extra cell or two (the fin) in one box.

Either a fin holds the digit, or it doesn't, and then the X-Wing is real. If the X-Wing is real, its two columns get the digit from the two rows and nowhere else. If a fin holds it, every cell of the fin's box loses it. So a cell that is in one of the X-Wing's columns and also in the fin's box loses the digit either way.

A sashimi X-Wing is the same with one corner of the X-Wing missing: one row has the digit in only one of the two columns, plus the fin. The reasoning, and the cells it clears, are the same.

Rows and columns can swap roles, as with every fish.
